== Description ==

**Codiepress Cart Rewards** is the most flexible reward rules engine for WooCommerce. Build automated reward campaigns that deliver free shipping, order discounts, and free products — triggered instantly when a customer's cart meets your conditions, with no coupon codes required.

Traditional promotions put the burden on the customer. They forget the code, mistype it, find it on a deal site, and then email you when it doesn't work. **Codiepress Cart Rewards eliminates every one of those friction points.** Rewards apply automatically, the moment a customer qualifies — creating a seamless experience that converts better and feels more generous.

> 🎯 **Set your conditions. Define your rewards. Codiepress Cart Rewards runs the rest — automatically.**

Codiepress Cart Rewards is built for WooCommerce stores that want to do more than hand out coupon codes. With a powerful rule-based engine, you can target rewards with surgical precision: by cart value, item count, weight, volume, specific products, categories, tags, or shipping classes. Each reward can bundle multiple reward types together — free shipping, a discount, and a free product all triggered by a single rule.

And with **reward lines**, you can go even further. Create multiple delivery lines within a single reward type to build tiered campaigns and progressive incentives — Codiepress Cart Rewards automatically applies the best matching line, so customers always get the most relevant reward.

---

### How Codiepress Cart Rewards Works

**Step 1 — Create a Reward**
Name your reward and select which reward types it should include: Free Shipping, Order Discount, Free Product, or any combination.

**Step 2 — Configure Reward Lines**
For each reward type, configure one or more reward lines. Each line defines the specific details of that reward — the discount amount, the free product, or the shipping method. In the Pro version, multiple reward lines per type allow tiered and progressive reward structures.

**Step 3 — Define Conditions**
Set the conditions that must be met for the reward to trigger. Choose from cart subtotal, quantity, weight, volume, specific products, variations, categories, tags, shipping classes, and excluded products. Organize conditions into groups with AND/OR logic for advanced targeting.

**Step 4 — Publish**
Your reward goes live. Codiepress Cart Rewards monitors every cart in real time and applies qualifying rewards automatically. Reward badges keep customers informed of available and upcoming rewards throughout their shopping journey.

== Reward Types ==

= 🚚 Free Shipping =

The Free Shipping reward type automatically removes shipping costs from the cart the moment a customer's cart satisfies your conditions. No code required — the charge simply disappears.

Free shipping is one of the most powerful conversion levers in eCommerce. Research consistently shows it outperforms equivalent monetary discounts in driving purchase decisions. Codiepress Cart Rewards lets you deploy it with precision: target it to a specific cart subtotal, item count, weight, product category, or any combination of conditions you define.

**Free Shipping reward lines** let you configure multiple free shipping entries within a single reward type. When more than one line is eligible, Codiepress Cart Rewards automatically applies the most beneficial one — preventing duplicate rewards while giving you the flexibility to build tiered shipping campaigns.

**Perfect for:** Subtotal-based "spend $X, ship free" campaigns, weight-based shipping incentives, category-specific free shipping promotions, and high-volume order rewards.

= 💰 Order Discount =

The Order Discount reward type applies a fixed-amount or percentage-based discount to the cart total — automatically, with no coupon code required. The discount appears on the cart and checkout pages just as a coupon would, but is applied entirely by your reward rule logic.

**Order Discount reward lines** allow you to configure multiple discount entries within a single reward type. When more than one line is eligible, Codiepress Cart Rewards automatically applies the highest discount amount — enabling tiered discount campaigns without the risk of duplicate discounts stacking unintentionally.

**Perfect for:** Volume-based discounts, category and tag-driven promotions, high-value cart incentives, event-driven sales, and tiered pricing campaigns.

= 🎁 Free Product =

The Free Product reward type automatically adds a specific product to the customer's cart at zero cost when they qualify. You choose the product, and for variable products, you specify the exact variation to be gifted. The item appears in the cart automatically — no customer input needed.

**Free Product reward lines** let you configure multiple free product entries within a single reward type. When more than one line is eligible, Codiepress Cart Rewards automatically applies the most valuable offer — making it easy to create tiered gift campaigns where the best matching free product is always the one the customer receives.

**Perfect for:** Buy-X-get-Y-free promotions, gift-with-purchase campaigns, product sample giveaways, new product introductions, and multi-gift bundle rewards.


== Reward Lines ==

Reward lines are individual delivery entries within a reward type. Each reward type — Free Shipping, Order Discount, and Free Product — can contain one or more reward lines, and each line can be configured independently.

= What Reward Lines Enable =

In the free version, each reward type supports one reward line — giving you a clean, straightforward setup for single-tier promotions. Codiepress Cart Rewards Pro unlocks multiple reward lines per reward type, enabling:

**Tiered Discounts** — Configure a $5 discount on one line and a $10 discount on another within the same Order Discount reward type. Codiepress Cart Rewards evaluates the lines and applies the best one for the customer.

**Multiple Free Products** — Add several Free Product lines to a single reward, each targeting a different product or variation. Codiepress Cart Rewards evaluates all eligible lines and applies the most valuable one — ideal for tiered gift campaigns where the best offer always wins.

**Advanced Free Shipping** — Configure multiple Free Shipping lines targeting different shipping scenarios or methods within a single reward rule.

= Practical Examples =

* An Order Discount reward with two lines: a 10% discount line and a $5 discount line — Codiepress Cart Rewards evaluates both and applies the best one for the customer.
* A Free Product reward with three lines, each targeting a different product — Codiepress Cart Rewards evaluates all eligible lines and adds the most valuable free product to the cart automatically.
* A tiered Free Shipping reward with lines targeting different shipping method configurations based on cart composition.

Reward lines give Pro users the flexibility to build sophisticated, multi-layered promotional campaigns without needing to create separate reward rules for each delivery component.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I create my first reward? =

After activating Codiepress Cart Rewards, go to **Codiepress Cart Rewards** in your WordPress admin menu and click **Add New Reward**. Give your reward a name, select one or more reward types, configure your reward lines, add your conditions, and publish. Your reward will begin applying automatically the moment a customer's cart qualifies.

= Do customers need to enter a coupon code to receive rewards? =

No. By default, Codiepress Cart Rewards applies rewards automatically when the cart meets your defined conditions. Coupon codes are entirely optional — each reward line can optionally be tied to a specific coupon code if you want that line to activate only when a customer has applied it.

= Can a single reward include multiple reward types? =

Yes. A single reward rule can bundle Free Shipping, an Order Discount, and a Free Product together. When the rule triggers, all enabled reward types are applied. Within each reward type, Codiepress Cart Rewards evaluates all configured lines and applies only the best matching one. The free version supports up to 2 reward types per rule; Pro supports unlimited.

= Does the Free Product reward work with variable products? =

Yes. When configuring a Free Product reward line, you can select a variable product and choose the exact variation to be added as the free gift. Each Free Product reward line can target a different product or variation.

= What happens if a customer no longer qualifies for a reward? =

Codiepress Cart Rewards monitors the cart in real time. If a customer removes items and their cart no longer meets the conditions, the reward is automatically removed. Rewards are re-applied immediately if conditions are met again — all without any page refresh or customer action.

= Is Codiepress Cart Rewards compatible with my WooCommerce theme? =

Codiepress Cart Rewards is built on native WooCommerce APIs and follows WordPress coding standards, ensuring broad theme compatibility. Core reward logic does not depend on theme-specific markup. If you encounter a display issue with badges or messages in a specific theme, please contact support.
